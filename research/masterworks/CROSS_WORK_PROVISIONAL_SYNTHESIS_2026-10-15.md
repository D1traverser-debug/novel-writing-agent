## 1. Why this comparison exists now

The previous study policy deferred all cross-work synthesis until the entire corpus queue completed. That protected against premature promotion, but it also delayed feedback: the system could read twenty-plus books with a biased observation frame and only discover the bias at the end.

This bootstrap comparison changes the feedback topology without changing the user's one-book-at-a-time requirement:

- sequential full-work study remains one work at a time;
- completed works are compared after each completion;
- comparison is explicitly non-promotional while the campaign is active;
- conflicts and blind spots update what the next work should be observed for;
- final rule promotion and zero-based story reselection remain blocked until the campaign's formal synthesis gate.

## 2. Repeated mechanisms across independent completed families

### M001 — ANSWER_EXPANDS_MODEL
Across the completed works, strong reveals often do more than close a question. They force a higher-level model of the story:
- a local game/incident becomes part of a larger social or world system;
- a case or clue is reinterpreted inside a broader causal network;
- an apparent explanation remains provisional because source credibility or missing information still matters.

Boundary: endless question replacement without meaningful payoff becomes mystery debt, not depth.

### M002 — BOUNDED_EPISTEMIC_COMPETENCE
Compelling intelligent protagonists are not simply correct. Their competence is bounded by information, domain expertise, cost and revisability. They form models, act, get contradicted and update.

Boundary: if every uncertainty exists only so the protagonist can demonstrate being one step smarter, ensemble agency and suspense flatten.

### M003 — DISTRIBUTED_OPERATIONAL_INTELLIGENCE
Ensembles remain useful when different characters control different forms of knowledge, access, judgment, risk tolerance or action capacity. This creates dependence and disagreement instead of a protagonist plus exposition terminals.

Boundary: distinct dialogue voices do not compensate for identical decision functions.

### M004 — WORLD_RULES_MUST_CHANGE_ACTION
Rules, lore, profession, history and worldbuilding earn page cost when they alter routes, choices, risks, resources, relationships or hypotheses. Information density is not itself depth.

Boundary: explanatory accuracy can still slow the reading experience when it does not change the next meaningful action.

### M005 — RELATIONSHIP_ADHESION_REQUIRES_COST
Durable relationships strengthen through shared risk, rescue, debt, trust revision, disagreement and independent agency—not by declaring closeness or increasing banter frequency.

Boundary: repeated rescue without reciprocal cost can turn a mysterious/competent character into an automatic solution device.

### M006 — ENGINE_ROTATION_WITH_CONTINUITY
Long serials sustain attention by changing the local engine—game, investigation, travel, organization, experiment, relationship, political pressure, survival—while preserving larger promises and character continuity.

Boundary: rotation that breaks the reader contract feels like genre reset rather than evolution.

## 3. Negative evidence and contradictions

### N001 — PROCEDURAL_RIGOR_CAN_REDUCE_READING_MOMENTUM
The same epistemic discipline that makes a world believable can generate repetitive verification loops, explanation after the reader already understands, and memo/report texture. FQ001's rejected first-signing window is a live project example of this risk.

### N002 — CENTRAL_GENIUS_CAN_DESTROY_ENSEMBLE VALUE
High-competence protagonists create pleasure, but repeated `everyone stuck -> protagonist explains -> everyone follows` patterns reduce uncertainty and make secondary characters replaceable.

### N003 — MYSTERY_DEBT_NEEDS PAYOFF ACCOUNTING
Reframing and deeper unknowns are powerful only if some promises are actually paid. A system that optimizes only for opening questions will accumulate debris.

### N004 — REALISM IS A TOOL, NOT THE PRODUCT
Institutional, spatial or technical realism is useful when it changes consequence or choice. Exact timestamps, routes, procedures and access logic can become cognitive homework when overrepresented.

### N005 — SURFACE GENRE ELEMENTS ARE NOT TRANSFERABLE MECHANISMS
Rule games, tombs, occult systems, secret organizations, first-person adventure voice and specific power systems are evidence contexts, not reusable answers for FQ001.

## 4. What the FQ001 signing rejection changes

The platform rejection is not proof that the premise is invalid and not evidence of AI detection. It is a real external quality signal that the first-signing packet failed the platform's editorial quality bar.

The old system correctly diagnosed a weighting error: it over-optimized causal verification, continuity safety, access realism and timeline/spatial precision. The cross-work comparison sharpens that diagnosis:

- FQ001 had M002/M004 in excess;
- it underused M003/M005/M006 early enough;
- therefore the repair target is not 'less logic' but a better portfolio of reader rewards.

This remains a historical-project diagnosis. It must not privilege the current premise after the study campaign; the old concept competes from zero during story reselection.

## 5. Observation axes added for the remaining corpus

Every remaining work should now be observed for at least these comparative axes in addition to its book-specific target:

1. first-screen and first-20k reader-contract conversion;
2. time-to-first durable relationship engine;
3. payoff density versus mystery-debt growth;
4. local engine rotation versus identity continuity;
5. emotional aftershock after high-intensity events;
6. protagonist competence versus secondary-character agency;
7. explanation density and page-cost control;
8. how ordinary-life texture carries attachment rather than only realism;
9. how mid-series fatigue is prevented or recovered from;
10. ending pressure: how the intended ending constrains middle choices;
11. what breaks when the mechanism is removed or inverted;
12. whether the mechanism survives transfer to a materially different genre.
