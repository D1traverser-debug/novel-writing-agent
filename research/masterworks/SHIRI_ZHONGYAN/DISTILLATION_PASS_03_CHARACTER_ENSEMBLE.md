## 0. Question

How does a 3.2M-character puzzle-heavy serial keep side characters from becoming tools, and where does the same method create repetition or protagonist-centralization risk?

Target competencies: C04 character arc, C05 relationship/ensemble, C12 voice/subtext, C14 emotion/attachment, C15 memory/payoff, C20 learning.

## 1. Character depth is pre-computed before it is revealed

Public creation material reports that the author prepared detailed character biographies/timelines before or during early planning, and later chose to surface some biographies when readers questioned motives. The biography therefore was not merely decorative bonus material; it supplied a hidden causal model for speech and choice.

Distilled mechanism:
`private life model -> present behavior -> reader questions behavior -> selective backstory recontextualizes behavior`

This is stronger than:
`character behaves strangely -> author invents tragic past afterward to justify it`.

FQ001 transfer benchmark:
For every durable core character, maintain a private decision model that can predict at least three different pressure responses before drafting the scene. Backstory may remain off-page; it only enters prose when it changes current interpretation, leverage, trust or choice.

## 2. Biography chapters work because they reclassify existing evidence

A recurring device in the work is later first-person/character-centered life material that revisits an earlier surface identity or testimony. Its strongest function is retrospective reclassification:
- a line previously read as arrogance becomes defense;
- a decision previously read as irrational gains a private objective;
- a comic or functional side character acquires an independent moral history.

Key principle:
**Attachment grows when new information changes the meaning of behavior the reader already remembers.**

This is different from simply giving every supporting character a sad chapter.

Risk:
If every important character receives the same autobiography-shaped depth mechanism, the device itself becomes predictable. The system must separate `character depth` from `biography chapter`.

## 3. Independent agency is the core of ensemble writing

High-signal commentary praises the periods where multiple factions/characters have their own limited information, objectives and tactics rather than existing to make the protagonist look intelligent.

Ensemble audit variables:
- independent objective;
- private information;
- current belief/model;
- resource/leverage;
- relationship debts;
- action taken if protagonist is absent;
- consequence that returns to mainline.

FQ001 benchmark:
For Xu Zhiwei, Gu Xingzhou and Lao Ma, answer: “If Chen Du disappeared for one chapter, what would this person actively do next, and how could that action alter the mainline when Chen returns?”

A supporting character who can only wait, answer a question or be rescued fails durable-agency review.

## 4. Character familiarity is a production variable

The author’s post-completion reflection says later prose, emotional progression and character shaping became stronger as he became more familiar with the cast; later characters could produce surprising-but-consistent dialogue/actions.

NOVEL_OS should not rely on writing 500 chapters before characters become real. It needs a pre-draft simulation benchmark.

### Character simulation benchmark
Give a character one pressure situation unrelated to the planned plot and generate three plausible responses:
- plot-convenient response;
- stereotype/trait-sheet response;
- drive/relationship/history-consistent response.

A reviewer must identify why the preferred response is:
- not the most obvious move;
- still retrospectively inevitable for this person;
- capable of changing another character’s plan.

If every response can be predicted from one adjective (“冷静”“善良”“聪明”), the model is too thin.

## 5. Voice comes from value and strategy, not decoration

Distinct character voice is produced by differences in:
- what a person notices;
- what they refuse to discuss;
- what they consider evidence;
- what they joke about under pressure;
- which social risk they tolerate;
- how directly they ask for trust;
- what they do after being contradicted.

Voice benchmark for FQ001:
Remove names/speaker labels from a high-pressure exchange. A reviewer should recognize Chen Du/Xu Zhiwei/Gu Xingzhou primarily from goal, logic, avoidance and relationship posture, not catchphrases.

## 6. Attachment object can differ from ontology

Public discussion around the work shows a crucial long-serial lesson: readers may become attached to a particular lived version/state of a person even if the world model says later versions are “the same entity.” Logical continuity cannot automatically substitute for emotional continuity.

General rule candidate:
When speculative mechanics reset, copy, branch, overwrite or replace a person, audit two separate ledgers:
- `ONTOLOGICAL_CONTINUITY`: what the world rules say is the same entity;
- `ATTACHMENT_CONTINUITY`: which memories, relationships and lived history the reader is actually mourning/protecting.

FQ001 relevance:
If Mingrida later creates branch futures or mutually exclusive versions of events, the emotional question must not be reduced to deviation-rate mechanics. Readers need to know what human continuity is being lost.

## 7. Human “bottom” gives high-concept characters weight

Author interviews repeatedly connect the work’s people to varied real-life observation/experience. The useful lesson is not “suffer before writing.” It is:
- character stakes need ordinary material life;
- jobs, money, family obligations, shame, habits and social power change what an abstract rule means;
- a character should have a life the high concept interrupts.

FQ001 correction:
Chen Du cannot be only “future-order solver.” Lao Ma cannot be only “ordinary-life anchor” in outline terms. Xu Zhiwei cannot be only “falsification partner.” Their ordinary constraints must generate independent decisions.

## 8. Emotional progression: pressure needs memory and aftershock

Character attachment grows through repeated change of relationship state rather than isolated tragedy.

Track per durable relationship:
`trust / debt / power / knowledge asymmetry / intimacy / suspicion / moral disagreement`

A major emotional event should alter at least one of these and have later behavioral consequences.

Anti-pattern:
`trauma scene -> reader cries -> system resets -> relationship functions exactly as before`.

FQ001 experiment:
For each candidate CH1–CH8, identify one relationship delta that survives into the next chapter. If three chapters pass with all core relationships unchanged, the packet risks puzzle-machine detachment.

## 9. Side-story / biography insertion test

Before inserting a character-centered backstory sequence, require at least two of:
- resolves a current motive contradiction;
- reinterprets remembered behavior;
- changes present trust/leverage;
- creates a new choice;
- pays a planted promise;
- changes the mainline’s next action.

If its only function is “make this character more tragic/likable,” keep the material in private character history or compress it.

## 10. Negative-space findings

Success does not erase risks visible in public criticism:
- protagonist intelligence can eventually re-centralize a previously distributed ensemble;
- multiple parties with limited information can become “mystery people talking around facts” if no one updates the model;
- repeated biographies can become emotional formula;
- repeated suffering can feel engineered if relationship consequences do not diversify;
- a symbolic off-stage relationship can remain emotionally thin if it never gains independent agency.

These are useful because they identify the boundary where a strength becomes a defect.

## 11. FQ001 transfer candidates

### EXPERIMENTAL
- `CHARACTER_DECISION_SIMULATION` before high-impact scenes;
- `ABSENT_PROTAGONIST_AGENCY_TEST` for durable side characters;
- `RELATIONSHIP_DELTA_LEDGER` per chapter/packet;
- `ONTOLOGY_VS_ATTACHMENT_CONTINUITY` when future-branch mechanics appear;
- `BACKSTORY_PRESENT_CONSEQUENCE_GATE`;
- `VOICE_BY_VALUE_AND_AVOIDANCE` benchmark.

### NOT PROMOTED
- mandatory first-person biography chapters;
- mandatory tragic backstories;
- protagonist-as-secret-mastermind as a universal retention tool;
- reader popularity as sufficient reason to increase screen time;
- “every side character deserves equal chapters.”
