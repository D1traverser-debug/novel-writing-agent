## CH1 — 《明天的我，给今天的我下了一单》

Priority: `P1`

What works:
- immediately establishes Chen Du's occupation and urban-night texture;
- core concept is strong and concrete;
- death-time document produces a clean chapter-level promise;
- system anomaly is narrow rather than omnipotent.

Risk:
- normal-delivery setup before the anomaly is somewhat longer than necessary for a signing-window opening;
- early money/route details support realism but compete with first-screen concept conversion;
- emotional reaction remains deliberately restrained, which is in character but slightly lowers attachment velocity.

Repair target:
- compress the ordinary-delivery prelude while preserving one or two character-revealing details;
- surface the anomaly earlier in the reading experience;
- retain the `23:46 death time` payoff and South Ting Road proof;
- add no extra exposition or system explanation.

Forbidden repair:
- opening with an abstract lore dump;
- adding melodramatic panic inconsistent with Chen Du;
- giving Mingrida new powers.

## CH2 — 《已签收，待结算》

Priority: `P0`

What works:
- `已签收 / 待结算` meaningfully expands the system mystery;
- the 168-yuan ordinary order creates a bridge from supernatural information to real-world action;
- Lao Ma adds human texture.

Risk:
- extended institutional phone verification reads like an evidence-gathering sequence;
- several questions produce precise factual answers in succession;
- the chapter's dramatic center arrives late: the 168-yuan order and six-minute relationship to the death time.

Repair target:
- compress the funeral-service verification to the minimum facts that change Chen Du's decision: template is plausible, current system has no matching serial, cabinet records inaccessible;
- move narrative weight toward Chen Du deciding whether to accept the 168-yuan order;
- preserve the privacy/access refusal because it supports project reality.

Expected gain: faster mainline conversion, less report texture, stronger choice emphasis.

## CH3 — 《七号高架不是一个点》

Priority: `P0`

What works:
- concrete near-miss creates physical danger;
- temporary characters have their own motives;
- Chen Du changes the environment and realizes interventions move other routes too.

Risk:
- K13 markers, road geometry and worker dialogue occupy too much bandwidth;
- multiple explanatory beats restate the same spatial-risk inference;
- Chen Du's competence is again primarily shown through inspection/verification.

Repair target:
- keep one stable spatial anchor and the near-miss;
- reduce coordinate/marker repetition;
- strengthen the human consequence of Chen Du choosing to intervene despite uncertainty;
- let worker conflict and inconvenience carry more of the exposition.

Expected gain: preserve realism while converting logistics into drama.

## CH4 — 《有人在等这六分钟》

Priority: `P0-HIGHEST`

What works:
- the anonymous call enforcing `不要提前送达` is an excellent escalation;
- the chapter clarifies that timing is being actively controlled by a person, not merely by platform metadata.

Risk:
- daytime return to the station + return to the high bridge repeats the previous verification engine;
- too much procedural scouting occurs before the anonymous-call payoff;
- information is correct but the chapter feels like a second investigation pass over already established terrain.

Repair target:
- aggressively compress station and bridge re-verification;
- retain only information that changes the 23:40/23:46 choice;
- bring the anonymous-call intrusion earlier;
- increase interpersonal suspicion/friction with the Shunchi driver instead of adding more route analysis.

Expected gain: largest likely improvement per continuity cost in the current front arc.

## Cross-arc fixes

### X1 — Relationship/attachment velocity (`P0`)
Use Lao Ma more deliberately in CH1–CH5 as a durable human anchor: debt, teasing, ordinary care, inconvenience, disagreement. The goal is not more dialogue; it is earlier evidence that Chen Du's survival matters inside a social world.

### X2 — Procedural-density budget (`P0`)
For front-arc repair, exact timestamps/route markers/institutional verification survive only when they change one of:
- choice;
- danger;
- relationship;
- irreversible consequence;
- fair-play payoff.

Otherwise compress or move off-page.

### X3 — Choice-before-explanation (`P0`)
When a scene already has enough information for a meaningful choice, stop gathering and force the choice. Do not allow the protagonist to keep reducing uncertainty merely because the author can reason further.

### X4 — Signing-evaluation lens (`P0`)
Before the second signing application, review the repaired front arc explicitly on:
1. opening speed and concept conversion;
2. pacing / repeated scene engines;
3. protagonist and supporting-character attachment;
4. immersion vs report texture;
5. mainline clarity and escalation.

No numerical average may override a `HIGH` finding in one of these dimensions.
