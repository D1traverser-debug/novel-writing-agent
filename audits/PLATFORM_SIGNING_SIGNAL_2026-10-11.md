## 3. Manuscript audit against official signing dimensions

Scope reviewed: current authoritative CH1–CH8 FINAL text, i.e. approximately the first-signing-window material.

### 3.1 Opening — strength exists, but first-screen efficiency is not maximal

Strengths:
- CH1 has a clear high-concept hook: a future order addressed from/for Chen Du and a death-time document;
- the supernatural aperture is narrow and concrete;
- the protagonist occupation is immediately grounded in urban reality.

Risk:
- the chapter spends a meaningful opening span establishing normal delivery work before the Mingrida anomaly becomes the dominant engine;
- this realism is useful, but at signing-review speed the first-screen promise could be more compressed and emotionally sharper.

Assessment: `MEDIUM_RISK`, not the primary defect.

### 3.2 Pacing — highest-confidence risk

CH2–CH4 repeatedly use a similar engine:
- inspect/verify a location or institutional fact;
- ask staff or local workers for procedural details;
- cross-check time/route/position;
- revise the risk hypothesis;
- end on another operational uncertainty.

This is logically rigorous, but the cumulative reader experience is more procedural than escalating. The project had already identified a historical `query -> verification -> record -> summary` isomorphism; the current platform rejection makes that old concern materially relevant again.

CH6–CH8 improve character/world friction, but still retain high densities of exact time, vehicle/location state, access reasoning and fact-verification dialogue.

Assessment: `HIGH_RISK`.

### 3.3 Character setup / attachment — competent protagonist, delayed emotional/relational anchor

Chen Du is legible: practical, risk-averse, route/logistics smart, emotionally restrained. However, in the first-signing window much of his page-time demonstrates competence through analysis and verification. The reader gets less early relational or emotional leverage than the concept itself deserves.

Lao Ma provides human texture, but the first major continuing relationship that can carry tension, trust and disagreement—Xu Zhiwei—does not enter until CH8. That is late for a platform evaluation that explicitly includes character setup and immersion.

Assessment: `MEDIUM_HIGH_RISK`.

### 3.4 Immersion — strong concrete reality, weakened by report-like density

Strengths:
- urban infrastructure, night-shift work, hospital/parking/road friction and ordinary secondary characters feel concrete;
- the manuscript avoids easy omniscient access and respects real-world permissions.

Risk:
- frequent exact timestamps, road markers, route alternatives, vehicle positions, procedural confirmations and analytical transitions can create a report/memo texture;
- this directly conflicts with the project's own prose identity rule that the novel must not feel like a dispatch sheet or experiment log.

Assessment: `HIGH_RISK`.

### 3.5 Mainline — clear macro goal, but first arc spends too much bandwidth proving mechanics

The first order has a strong mainline: survive the stated death time and learn whether future information can be changed. The payoff at CH5 is functional and the second order continues the larger system mystery.

The risk is not mainline absence; it is mainline bandwidth allocation. Too much early text is spent validating how the mechanism maps onto routes, cabinets, maintenance access, road geometry and institutional procedures. Human consequence and relationship escalation receive comparatively less space.

Assessment: `MEDIUM_HIGH_RISK`.

## 4. Root-cause diagnosis

The novel system over-optimized the first 20k for:
- continuity correctness;
- causal verification;
- realistic access control;
- anti-plot-hole protection;
- exact timeline/spatial reasoning.

Those are valuable, but the optimization displaced platform-facing priorities:
- opening compression;
- scene-to-scene escalation;
- emotional/relational attachment;
- character high points;
- reduced procedural repetition;
- faster conversion from mystery information into irreversible human choice.

This is not a reason to abandon realism or the core concept. It is a weighting error in the early serialization objective function.

## 5. Decision after candidate challenge

Rejected: `continue unchanged to 50k and try again`.
Reason: the platform explicitly says quality should be improved before the next threshold, and the first evaluation is already negative evidence.

Rejected: `rewrite the entire book immediately`.
Reason: the current core concept, Canon logic and mainline are viable; broad rewrite would create unnecessary continuity risk.

Rejected: `assume AI detection and humanize by adding deliberate roughness/typos`.
Reason: no evidence supports an AI-detection cause; deliberate degradation conflicts with both craft and platform quality goals.

Rejected: `only improve CH9+`.
Reason: the second signing evaluation will still see the existing opening/front arc. New chapters cannot fully compensate for a weak early reader experience.

Accepted: `targeted front-arc signing repair before the 50k reapplication`, while preserving the Canon backbone.

## 6. Targeted repair strategy

Priority order:
1. re-audit CH1 first screen/opening compression without removing the core urban-reality identity;
2. structurally compress CH2–CH4 procedural verification loops and increase irreversible choice / interpersonal friction per scene;
3. strengthen early attachment to Chen Du via cost, vulnerability, ordinary-life stakes and Lao Ma relationship beats without turning him sentimental;
4. reduce timestamp/route/marker density where it does not change a decision;
5. preserve necessary domain reality but move non-decision-changing verification off-page or compress it;
6. evaluate whether Xu Zhiwei or another durable relationship signal needs earlier seeding, without forcing a continuity-breaking cameo;
7. keep CH5's payoff but improve the emotional aftershock and transition into the second-order arc;
8. review CH6–CH8 for the same procedural-density pattern, with lighter intervention than CH2–CH4 unless evidence demands more.

Every proposed front-arc change must preserve or explicitly migrate:
- Canon facts;
- timeline dependencies;
- foreshadow/payoff chains;
- CH5–CH8 downstream logic;
- published-chapter state and source-hash invalidation rules.

No platform replacement/upload is authorized by this audit. Revised chapter candidates may be prepared internally, but replacing already-published platform text remains an external high-impact action requiring the user's actual platform action/confirmation.
