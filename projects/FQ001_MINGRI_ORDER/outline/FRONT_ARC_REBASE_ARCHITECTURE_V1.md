## 0. Freeze meaning

This freezes the **functions, causal ordering, character/relationship duties and information boundaries** for the next candidate drafting pass. It does not freeze exact sentences, scene count, every timestamp, or final chapter word counts.

A Canon contradiction, domain-reality BLOCKER, or stronger architecture that survives explicit alternative/falsification may reopen this freeze. A new writing tip alone may not.

No candidate chapter becomes authoritative FINAL piecemeal. The packet promotes only after whole-packet gates pass.

# 1. Packet job

By the end of the first replacement packet, the reader should have experienced—not merely been told—that:

1. Mingrida can provide future information worth acting on;
2. Chen can alter a predicted future;
3. alteration produces migrated/ambiguous consequence rather than a guaranteed good ending;
4. an order condition can conflict with a real person’s refusal/agency;
5. Xu Zhiwei becomes a durable skeptical verification/consent pressure, not a one-scene helper;
6. Chen accepts at least one form of external falsification instead of owning the interpretation alone;
7. the second-order mystery points outward toward other actors/rules rather than closing as a simple rescue case.

Reader-contract balance at packet exit:
- first death promise: materially paid;
- second-order key/medicine promise: partially paid/reframed;
- Mingrida source/branch mystery: enlarged, not explained away;
- character relationship: permanently changed.

# 4. Chapter function contracts

## CH1 — Tomorrow wrote my death

### Required function
Convert premise into immediate personal stake and a forced action problem.

### Must happen
- ordinary night-work identity established economically;
- anomalous order appears early enough to dominate the chapter;
- sender/recipient/death-document contradiction is concrete;
- enough current-world corroboration destroys easy “simple prank” dismissal without becoming a research dossier;
- 23:46 death stake becomes actionable;
- Lao Ma or another ordinary-life anchor shows Chen has a life to lose.

### Character movement
Chen moves from dismissing anomaly to choosing to investigate/intervene despite uncertainty.

### End state
He has a specific survival problem and an incomplete evidence chain; next chapter is action, not generic research.

### Forbidden
- lore dump;
- convenient proof that makes doubt impossible;
- melodramatic personality transplant;
- ten-step checklist narration.

## CH2 — Ordinary order enters the death window

### Required function
Make the future threat collide with Chen’s real job and another human actor.

### Must happen
- 168 order enters;
- its time/place meaning overlaps the predicted death window;
- only minimum institutional/document verification survives;
- Lao Ma/real work pressure creates human/economic texture;
- anonymous “do not deliver early” interference appears before the chapter is mostly over;
- Chen must choose whether/how to take the order under uncertainty.

### Information effect
Reader now has evidence that a present-day actor may care about exact timing, but identity/motive remain open.

### End state
A consequential commitment pushes Chen toward the death window.

## CH3 — 23:46 payoff

### Required function
Pay the first major promise and immediately complicate its meaning.

### Must happen
- physical causal chain becomes real, not merely predicted;
- geography remains understandable with 1–2 stable anchors rather than coordinate homework;
- Chen’s earlier intervention matters but does not create omniscient control;
- 23:46 passes with Chen alive;
- survival has bodily/emotional/social aftershock;
- settlement/deviation/future credit appears with limited interpretation;
- second order opens before the packet loses momentum.

### End state
The reader knows future can change, but not whether the system wanted Chen alive or whether the change was “good.”

## CH4 — The recipient refuses

### Required function
Shift from survival puzzle to consent/responsibility conflict.

### Must happen
- spare-key / car mismatch is concrete;
- Qin Lang is reached by a realistic path;
- Qin explicitly refuses the key / renewed relation to the vehicle;
- Chen cannot solve the problem by saying “the order says you must take it”;
- Qin has local motive/agency independent of Chen.

### End state
Delivery condition and recipient agency are in direct conflict.

## CH5 — Who owns the responsibility

### Required function
Turn the key from object mystery into a live custody/vehicle/person consequence.

### Must happen
- current driver/user has legitimate practical concern;
- Chen is pressured to hand over / abandon / transfer responsibility conveniently;
- he chooses a traceable/non-convenient option with cost;
- vehicle/patient/medicine consequence enters through action rather than a fresh query chain.

### End state
Medicine/patient fact makes the second order materially larger than “who owns the key.”

## CH6 — Xu Zhiwei enters the durable engine

### Required function
Introduce a recurring human falsification pressure and prevent the medical plot from becoming protagonist-controlled research.

### Must happen
- medical duty/boundary remains primary for Xu;
- Qin’s old medicine label enters fairly;
- patient timeline shows illness began before the second order appeared;
- Chen reveals/utters Qin knowledge before label visibility in a way Xu can notice;
- Xu separates source-of-information from truth-of-fact;
- she does not accept Mingrida as explanation.

### Relationship delta
Xu now has reason to question Chen’s timing/source, and Chen cannot dismiss her as an NPC verifier.

## CH7 — Let someone falsify me

### Required function
Make Chen surrender some interpretive control and establish a reusable relationship protocol without writing an experiment report.

### Must happen
- Xu challenges post-hoc storytelling;
- a concrete future claim / fact class is selected for precommit or timestampable record;
- Chen decides how much to disclose and whether to let Xu into the chain;
- the choice costs privacy/control;
- their goals remain partly different.

### Relationship delta
They become a skeptical working pair, not believers in the same theory.

### Forbidden
- full supernatural confession accepted immediately;
- technical lecture about scientific method;
- magical evidence-sharing feature.

## CH8 — Second-order reframe / next-scale promise

### Required function
Use the new verification relationship against a live consequence, pay/reframe part of the second order, and point beyond the current case.

### Must happen
- at least one key/medicine/vehicle question receives a concrete partial answer or reinterpretation;
- that answer proves order success/moral goodness are not equivalent;
- Xu’s presence changes a decision/outcome, not just interpretation;
- an actor/rule anomaly expands the next question;
- packet closes with changed state, not “continue investigation tomorrow.”

### End state
Reader has completed one strong survival arc + one ethically ambiguous second-order arc and now expects competing actors/branches, not eight more copies of the same order investigation.

# 5. Cross-packet rhythm and relationship map

## Primary engines by chapter
1. anomaly + personal survival;
2. work/time interference + commitment;
3. physical causal payoff + aftershock;
4. refusal/consent;
5. custody/responsibility + consequence;
6. medical-boundary evidence + relationship collision;
7. trust/precommit choice;
8. second-order reframe + scale expansion.

Adjacent engine duplication is a HIGH risk if two chapters can be summarized by the same “Chen asks around until exact facts reveal the route.”

## Chen arc within packet
control by information → action under uncertainty → survival reinforces competence → refusal exposes limits → responsibility without ownership → external challenge → accepts falsification → learns changed future ≠ morally solved future.

## Xu arc within packet
professional stranger → detects impossible timing → demands evidence discipline → gains limited reason to cooperate → becomes independent decision pressure.

## Lao Ma function
Appear only where ordinary-life/emotional/social cost is real. Do not use him to explain Mingrida or solve research questions.

# 6. Mystery / fair-play packet map

Questions that must receive meaningful payment by packet end:
- Is the death prediction actionable? YES.
- Can it be changed? YES, with ambiguity.
- Does settlement indicate moral success? NO / not demonstrated.
- Is second order simply meant to prevent the patient’s earlier illness? NO, timing refutes this.
- Can recipient refusal matter against order condition? YES, creates conflict rather than auto-failure answer.

Questions deliberately left active:
- who arranged/cares about 168 timing;
- why future self can send after predicted death;
- what deviation/credit ultimately mean;
- why Qin was specified;
- who/what selects order outcomes;
- whether another actor knows the system.

No chapter may manufacture a final answer to the long ontology merely to sharpen a hook.
